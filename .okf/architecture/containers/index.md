C4 level 2 - what runs where.

# Concepts
* [App](app.md) - C4 level 2 - the main iOS application: targets, modules, entry point and what it talks to.
* [Backend](backend.md) - C4 level 2 - the servers the app depends on, as seen from the app.
* [Notification Service Extension](notification-service-extension.md) - C4 level 2 - the separate process that fetches and decrypts a pushed event and rewrites the notification.
* [Share Extension](share-extension.md) - C4 level 2 - the system share sheet entry that hands what another app shares to the app.
